swyr 0.2.2

The Command Bar now puts search first, with live suggestions as you type. Open tabs appear only when they strongly match what you typed.

Changed shortcuts now take effect everywhere, including on pages that use the same key.

Opt+1 to Opt+9 jump to your Favorites.

Folders and live folders now move with their contents.

Archived Split Views restore as splits.

Internal pages have their own icons in the sidebar.

History reopens local files.

The Library shows canvas thumbnails and a list of media you played earlier.

Private downloads stay private: they appear only in their own private window and leave the list when it closes.

Turning off a style with code undoes it.

Video and audio in embedded frames show the media controls.

swyr checks for updates again when it comes to the front.
